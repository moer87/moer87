About

Artist-led. Web3 focused.

My work combines pixel art, animation and hands-on product building, with a focus on original onchain projects and digital experiences.

MOE / MOER87

I’m Mohammadreza Eslami, known as MoeR87 — an independent pixel artist, animator and Web3 creator. I entered blockchain in 2018 and began releasing my own NFT work in 2022.

Since then, I’ve launched collections and collaborations across multiple networks, including Polygon Ape Punks, a 10,000-piece collection that sold out, while expanding my work into community systems, websites and AI-assisted Web3 products.

My current focus is Based Moer / Moerverse / Moe AI on Base, alongside H00DEXE on Robinhood Chain.

Journey

From pencil lines to persistent worlds.

This is the short version of a much longer path — the moments that changed what I made, how I worked, and what I believed digital ownership could become.

Childhood

Drawing first

Years of anatomy sketches and hand drawing built my foundation in form, proportion and movement.

2000s

Music journey

Independent music and digital publishing were part of my early creative work before Web3.

2018

Blockchain discovery

I became interested in ownership and persistence — digital objects existing beyond the platform that created them.

2020

NFTs through games

My first real NFT experience came from blockchain games and Enjin assets: collecting, trading and using digital objects as functional game items.

2022

Pixel artist onchain

I moved from collecting to creating, taught myself animation and released Famous Pixel Dogs alongside one-off works and experiments.

2023

Polygon Ape Punks

Created and launched a 10,000-piece Polygon collection that sold out, while also contributing animated pixel work to projects such as The Herd.

2024–25

Multi-chain experiments

Continued releasing and testing work across Ethereum, Polygon, Solana, Monad, Base and emerging testnets — including collections, free drops and experimental deployments.

2025–26

From collections to ecosystems

Based Moer expanded into the Moerverse and Moe AI on Base, while my current work also extended to H00DEXE on Robinhood Chain.

Background

Creative disciplines behind the work.

A concise look at the creative and technical influences that continue to shape how I approach Web3 projects.

Music

Music journey

Earlier independent music work continues to influence rhythm, pacing and presentation in my visual projects.

Games

MMORPG mindset

Persistent worlds, evolving characters, player economies and identity systems continue to influence how I think about digital communities.

Art

Hand-drawn first

Years of anatomy sketching came before digital art. Pixel work still follows the same rule: every mark should have a reason to exist.

Animation

Pixels in motion

I moved naturally from static pixel art into hand-built animation, especially for collections, character pieces and storytelling.

Web3

Ownership over hype

What interested me in blockchain was always the possibility of persistent digital objects and creator-owned systems, not speculation alone.

Building

Artist-first execution

I use modern tools and AI to help turn ideas into functioning sites and products, while keeping the visual identity, rules and creative direction my own.
