About Me

I'm Moe (MoeR87), a passionate pixel artist and Web3 creator with over a decade of experience in digital art. My journey began in the retro gaming scene, where I fell in love with the charm and limitations of pixel art. Today, I blend traditional pixel art techniques with modern Web3 technology to create unique digital collectibles and immersive experiences.

My work explores the intersection of nostalgia and innovation, bringing blocky pixels to life through animation and interactive elements. I collaborate with NFT platforms, game developers, and digital galleries to push the boundaries of what pixel art can be in the blockchain era.
