About Me

I design and animate pixel characters, collections, and interchangeable art systems, helping projects stand out through strong visual identity rather than hype.

Alongside the art, I lead and collaborate on NFT projects across different blockchains and emerging networks, bringing structure, clarity, and long-term vision to each build.

Pixel art is not just a style to me; it's a language. Every piece is crafted with intention, consistency, and respect for the culture that made pixel art iconic, while pushing it forward with animation and on-chain utility.
