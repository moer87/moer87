About

MoeR87 is a self-taught pixel artist and animator creating handcrafted, narrative-driven digital worlds. From early hand-drawn sketches of human anatomy to large-scale animated collections like Famous Pixel Dogs and Polygon Ape Punks, he has spent years mastering form, movement, and storytelling in pixels.

A lifelong gamer and early blockchain adopter, MoeR87 transforms concepts into immersive pixel art adventures across Ethereum, Polygon, Monad, and Base, including his ongoing Moerverse and Based Ape Punks projects.

Every character, story, and world is built entirely solo, one pixel at a time, blending artistry, animation, and digital ownership into cohesive, living universes.
